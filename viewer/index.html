Fields are computed once (quasi-static, linear): changing the current only rescales the colour bar. A pattern is the sum of the single-contact potentials weighted by their currents (superposition); its |E| is a finite difference on the grid (approximate next to the perineurium), its activating function the second difference along z over one internode. The pulse train is an illustration of I(t).
