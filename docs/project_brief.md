# Project brief (as given by the course)

**Simulating Electrical Stimulation of an Upper-Limb Peripheral Nerve**

The project assesses, by finite element simulation, whether the layout of the active sites of a
new intraneural electrode can stimulate all fascicles of the human median nerve.

## Specific objectives

- Develop a 3D finite element model of the median nerve.
- Simulate the electric field potential, voltage and charge generated by the electrode under
  different stimulation conditions.
- Determine which fibers are activated in each fascicle and the corresponding threshold currents.
- Verify compliance with tissue and electrode safety limits.
- Assess the limitations of the model.

## Phases

The project is organised in consecutive phases.

1. **Nerve geometry.** Reproducible generation of the nerve cross-section, complete with the main
   nerve structures.
2. **Electrical properties.** Selection and justification of tissue conductivities from the
   literature.
3. **Electrode and mesh.** Integration of the provided active sites and generation of an adequate
   3D mesh of the nerve.
4. **FEM solution.** Solution of the electrical problem with finite element (MATLAB PDE Toolbox)
   and verification of the results.
5. **Fiber activation.** Estimation of the activated regions and threshold currents in each
   fascicle, using a criterion based on the activating function.
6. **Results discussion.** Critical evaluation of the results across several virtual patients, in
   light of the model simplifications.

## Reference papers (provided)
