## 1. Where we are

- **Tutor's code** (`src/MatlabCode/PW_lesson_5_11_students.m`): a **2D** cross-section of the
  median nerve with 7 fixed fascicles (R = 1.5 mm), four tissues with conductivities from Davis
  et al. 2023, a triangular mesh and plots. No electrode, no solution yet.
- **Electrode layout** (`data/electrode/sites.mat`, described in `data/electrode/README.md`):
  48 gold active sites (AS), 8 sections along the nerve at z = ±4.3, ±6.3, ±8.3, ±10.3 mm. Each
  section: 4 intraneural AS on a shank crossing the nerve (r = 0.3 and 0.9 mm) + 2 cuff AS on
  the surface. The shank turns by 45° per section → only **4 distinct orientations**, each used
  twice (mirror image at ±z). Device length ≈ 21 mm.
- **Goal for the next tutor meeting:** add the AS to the model and simulate them.
- **Already done:** project skeleton (folders, conventions), literature notes for 10 papers
  (`docs/literature/`), parameter table (`docs/parameters.md`), the electrode-layout loader in
  SI units with 7 passing tests (`src/electrode/loadActiveSites.m`), MATLAB PDE Toolbox checks
  (`docs/architecture.md` §5).

## 2. Architecture (to approve)

Summary of `docs/architecture.md`:

- one folder of `src/` per module, one owner per module, communication only through fixed
  structs (`params`, `patient`, `sites`, `nerveModel`, `sol`, `fibres`, `thr`, `safety`);
- SI units in the code, all numbers in `config/default_params.m` with their source;
- **solve once per AS with 1 A and scale** (the problem is linear);
- geometry built from a 2D cross-section extruded along z, but **the electrical problem is solved
  in 3D**. Stacking independent 2D solutions does not work: it misses the current flowing along
  the nerve and the second derivative along z needed by the activating function
  (architecture §2).
- Function skeletons for every module are already in `src/` (they stop with a
  "not implemented" error and their header states the contract), so each person can start
  from their file.

## 3. Decisions needed to start

For each: options, a proposal, and who studies it if we cannot decide on Tuesday. Agreed choices
go into `docs/decisions.md`.

**D-a. Solver interface.** `femodel` (as in the tutor's script) accepts only isotropic σ;
`createpde` accepts σL ≠ σT (tested). *Proposal:* `createpde`, because the endoneurium
anisotropy (σL/σT ≈ 3.4) is exactly what shapes the field along the fibres.

**D-b. Cross-section for sprints 1–2.** The tutor's fixed 7 fascicles (patient P00) vs our own
generator straight away. *Proposal:* P00 first, so WP2–WP4 can start on Tuesday; the generator
(WP1) arrives in sprint 3. Note: with P00 the r = 0.3 mm AS cut through the perineurium of the
central fascicle (see D-g).

**D-c. Conductivity baseline.** The tutor's script uses Davis 2023 (ASCENT defaults: endoneurium
ρT = 6, ρL = 1.75 Ω·m, perineurium 1149 Ω·m, epineurium 6.3 Ω·m, muscle 0.086/0.35 S/m).
Alternatives: Zelechowski 2020 (model of the human-nerve TIME study we validate against), Horn
2023 (measured, canine). *Proposal:* keep Davis 2023 as baseline (consistent with the tutor),
the other two in the sensitivity analysis. Owner: WP5.

**D-d. Domain size and outer boundary.** The model must be longer than the device (≈ 21 mm) plus
margin; the outer surface can be grounded (V = 0) or insulated with a distant return electrode.
*Proposal:* WP3 tests both on the toy model in sprint 1 and reports how much the threshold
changes; meanwhile length ≈ 40 mm, outer radius 3·R as in the tutor's script.

**D-e. Electrode representation.** (1) AS as gold volumes (`addCell`) with current injected on
the face; (2) AS as holes (`addVoid`) with a current-density boundary condition on the face;
(3) is the **shank** modelled (insulating body carrying the intraneural sites)? *Proposal:* (2)
for the AS (simpler, standard), shank modelled as an insulating slab once the tutor gives its
size. Owner: WP2.

**D-f. Perineurium representation.** Explicit 20–40 µm layer → ≈ 10⁷ elements in 3D (too many).
Options: thicker layer with σ rescaled to keep the same ρ·t; thin-layer interface condition;
shorter model. *Proposal:* thicker layer with constant ρ·t (keeps the electrical barrier, cuts
the elements), checked against the thin layer in 2D. Owners: WP2 + WP5.

**D-g. AS crossing a perineurium.** With P00, the r = 0.3 mm AS (span r = 0.2–0.4 mm) cross the
perineurium of the central fascicle (radius 0.36/0.40 mm). Does the shank pierce the fascicle or
push it aside? Ask the tutor; meanwhile *proposal:* move P00's central fascicle so that it does
not cross an AS, or let the AS sit inside the endoneurium (both documented).

**D-h. Implantation level.** Upper arm (≈ 12 fascicles, larger) vs proximal forearm (≈ 22 small
fascicles), Delgado-Martínez 2016. The tutor's R = 1.5 mm corresponds to the proximal forearm
area (7.13 mm²). *Proposal:* proximal forearm. Owner: WP1.

Still open, not needed for sprint 1: activation criterion and AF threshold (WP4), number of
virtual patients and what varies between them (WP1), circular vs elliptical nerve (eccentricity
0.65 in Delgado-Martínez, WP1).

## 5. Questions for the tutor (draft)

1. **AS thickness:** `sites.mat` gives 0.005 mm (5 µm), the slides give 50 µm × 200 µm × 200 µm.
   Which one is right?
2. **Shank:** width, thickness and material of the shank carrying the intraneural AS? Should it be
   modelled?
3. **Active faces:** does current leave both square faces of an intraneural AS, or only one? For
   cuff AS, only the face on the nerve?
4. **Shank vs fascicles:** does the shank pierce the fascicles it crosses, or are they assumed to be
   pushed aside?
5. **Return electrode / ground:** where is it in the real set-up (distant, or one of the AS)?
6. **Stimulation:** monopolar only, or also bipolar between AS? Which pulse width?
7. **Deliverable:** report, presentation, code? Deadline? Dates of the next meetings?
8. The loader `load_active_sites.m` refers to `active_sites.mat`, but the file sent is
   `sites.mat`: same data, just a different name?
