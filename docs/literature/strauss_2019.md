# Strauss et al. 2019 — Multi-channel intraneural stimulation in transradial amputees

**Citation.** Strauss I, Valle G, Artoni F, D'Anna E, Granata G, Di Iorio R, et al.
*Characterization of multi-channel intraneural stimulation in transradial amputees.* Sci. Rep.
9:19258, 2019. doi:10.1038/s41598-019-55591-z. Open access: PMC6917705.

**Role in the project.** Human TIME threshold data besides the 5.39 nC quoted by Zelechowski 2020
(validation target and input to the safety check; issue N3 in `Papers/main.tex`).

## Numbers

- 4 transradial amputees, each with 4 TIMEs: 2 in the median nerve, 2 in the ulnar.
- Biphasic, charge-balanced, cathodic-first pulses; amplitude 10–980 µA, pulse width 20–120 µs,
  50 Hz, 2-s trains.
- Minimum charge evoking a sensation, average over subjects: **37.72 nC per active site** with
  single-channel stimulation; 16.58 nC per site with two channels together (sensory facilitation).

## Caveats

- Perceptual threshold (sensation), not 10%-of-fascicle recruitment; averaged over sites and
  sessions; time since implant not extracted here.
- AS size of the implanted TIMEs not checked: needed before turning charge into charge density.
